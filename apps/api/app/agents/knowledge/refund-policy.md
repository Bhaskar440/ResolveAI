# Northwind Supply Co. — Refund Policy

This policy governs when the merchant may issue a refund without a prior replacement
attempt, and how much may be refunded.

## Refund authority and timelines

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 3-5 business days.
Refunds up to 50.00 USD may be issued autonomously when evidence is strong. Refunds
above 50.00 USD require a human operator. The refundable amount can never exceed the
captured payment, and the currency must match the capture currency.

Tags: refund, partial refund, approval, authority, timeline, capture, autonomous

## Full refunds

A full refund is appropriate when the buyer paid but received nothing, when the parcel
is presumed lost, or when the merchant cannot supply a replacement for a confirmed
damaged item. The amount is the captured item price plus any tax and shipping that the
buyer paid. The refund must not exceed the captured amount.

Tags: full refund, lost parcel, not received, captured amount, tax, shipping

## Partial refunds

A partial refund is appropriate when the item arrived damaged or materially different
but remains usable, or when the buyer and merchant agree to keep the item. The merchant
may offer 30-60% of the item price depending on severity and resale value. A partial
refund does not require the buyer to return the item unless the offer says so.

Tags: partial refund, damaged, not as described, severity, resale value, keep item

## Refund eligibility checks

Before proposing a refund the merchant must verify the capture exists, the capture
status is completed, the captured amount and currency match the dispute, and the
transaction has not already been refunded. Duplicate refunds are prohibited. The
merchant must never refund more than the remaining refundable balance.

Tags: eligibility, capture, completed, currency, duplicate refund, refundable balance

## Refund approvals

Refunds above the merchant's autonomous cap require human approval before any money
moves. The agent may prepare the refund plan and record the reason, but it must not
execute the refund. Approval must include the amount, currency, decision code and the
policy citations that justify the decision.

Tags: human approval, cap, approval, refund plan, citations
