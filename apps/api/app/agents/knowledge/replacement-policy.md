# Northwind Supply Co. — Product Replacement Policy

This policy governs replacement offers before a refund is considered.

## Replacements and returns

Replacements are offered before refunds for cosmetic or partial damage. The buyer may
decline a replacement. Return shipping is paid by the merchant when the item is damaged
or not as described, and by the buyer for change-of-mind returns.

Tags: replacement, return, shipping cost, change of mind, damaged

## Replacement eligibility

A replacement is available when the item is damaged, defective, or materially different
from the listing, and the merchant has replacement stock. The merchant must offer the
same model or an equivalent model with the buyer's consent. The buyer may decline the
replacement and request a refund instead.

Tags: replacement eligibility, damaged, defective, material difference, stock, equivalent model

## Replacement parts

For cosmetic or partial damage the merchant may first offer replacement parts. If the
parts do not resolve the issue, the buyer may request a full replacement or a refund.
The merchant must provide instructions and pay for shipping of replacement parts.

Tags: replacement parts, cosmetic damage, partial damage, instructions, shipping cost

## Replacement timelines

Replacements ship within two business days of approval. If the merchant cannot ship a
replacement within ten business days, the buyer may request a full refund instead. The
merchant must not charge the buyer for a replacement that is due to merchant fault.

Tags: replacement timeline, two business days, ten business days, full refund, merchant fault

## Replacement refusal

If the buyer declines a replacement, the merchant may offer a partial refund of 30-60%
for damaged or not-as-described items. A full refund requires the item to be returned
or photographic evidence that return is impractical. The merchant must document the
buyer's refusal.

Tags: refusal, partial refund, full refund, return, photo evidence, documentation
