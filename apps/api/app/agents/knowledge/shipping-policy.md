# Northwind Supply Co. — Shipping and Delivery Policy

This policy defines when an order counts as delivered and how the merchant handles
late, lost, or misdelivered parcels.

## Shipping and delivery

Orders ship within two business days. A parcel is considered delivered only when the
carrier records a delivery scan at the buyer's address. A label-created scan is not
proof of delivery. If tracking shows no carrier acceptance after ten days, the order
is presumed lost and the merchant refunds the item price in full.

Tags: MERCHANDISE_OR_SERVICE_NOT_RECEIVED, delivery, tracking, lost parcel, label created

## Dispatch timelines

The merchant must provide a valid tracking number when the order ships. If the merchant
cannot ship within two business days, it must contact the buyer and offer a revised
delivery date or a cancellation and full refund. Repeated dispatch delays are a service
failure and should be recorded.

Tags: dispatch, two business days, tracking number, delay, cancellation, service failure

## Delivery proof

A delivery scan must include a timestamp and the buyer's city or postal code. A scan at
a sorting facility is not proof of delivery to the buyer. If the carrier marks the
parcel delivered but the buyer reports it missing, the merchant must open a carrier
investigation and may offer a replacement or refund when the investigation does not
locate the parcel.

Tags: delivery proof, timestamp, postal code, sorting facility, carrier investigation, missing parcel

## Misdelivered parcels

When tracking shows delivery to the wrong address, the merchant must arrange a
replacement or refund after confirming the carrier error. The buyer must not be
required to recover the parcel. If the address was entered incorrectly by the buyer,
the merchant may offer a replacement at a reduced price.

Tags: misdelivered, wrong address, carrier error, replacement, refund, buyer address

## Shipping charges

Original shipping charges are refunded when the parcel is lost, never delivered, or
damaged in transit. Shipping charges are not refunded for change-of-mind returns unless
the merchant chooses to waive them.

Tags: shipping charges, refund, lost, damaged in transit, change of mind
