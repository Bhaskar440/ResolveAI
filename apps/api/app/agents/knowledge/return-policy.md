# Northwind Supply Co. — Return Policy

This policy governs returns, return shipping costs, and what the merchant may require
before refunding or replacing an item.

## Returns and return shipping

Buyers may return unused items within 14 days of delivery for a refund, excluding
original shipping unless the item was damaged or not as described. Return shipping is
paid by the merchant when the item is damaged or not as described, and by the buyer for
change-of-mind returns. The buyer must provide a return tracking number for any return
that the merchant pays for.

Tags: return, 14 days, return shipping, change of mind, damaged, tracking

## Damaged or not as described

Buyers must report damage within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the item and the
packaging. Where damage is confirmed, the merchant first offers replacement parts. If
the buyer declines a replacement, the merchant may offer a partial refund of 30-60%
depending on the severity and resale value. A full refund requires the damaged item to
be returned or photographic evidence that return is impractical.

Tags: MERCHANDISE_OR_SERVICE_NOT_AS_DESCRIBED, damaged, cracked, return, partial refund, photo

## Return condition requirements

Returned items must include all original parts and packaging. The merchant may deduct
the cost of missing parts from the refund. Items that show signs of use beyond normal
inspection may receive a reduced refund. The merchant must explain any deduction and
cite this policy section.

Tags: condition, parts, packaging, deduction, reduced refund, inspection

## Return exceptions

Personalised items, perishable goods, and hygiene products cannot be returned unless
they arrived damaged or defective. Digital goods are not returnable after delivery. If
an exception applies, the merchant must still consider a refund when the item was not
delivered or was materially different from the listing.

Tags: exceptions, personalised, perishable, hygiene, digital goods, not delivered

## Return verification

A return is complete only when the carrier records the return delivery scan at the
merchant's address, or when the merchant confirms receipt. The merchant must not refund
before the return is received unless the policy explicitly allows an advance refund for
a lost outbound parcel.

Tags: return verification, delivery scan, receipt, advance refund, lost parcel
